# Data provenance

## Current Laguna scope

- `barangays.geojson`: OCHA COD-AB Philippines v03, `phl_admin4`, valid 2025-02-13, PSA/NAMRIA via HDX, CC BY-IGO. `scripts/boundaries.py` extracts 682 barangays in 30 Laguna municipalities, simplifying geometry at 0.00001 degrees.
- The published primary replay covers Bay, Pila, Victoria and Santa Cruz (67 barangays) with 136 Sentinel-1 passes: 84 on descending track 32 and 52 on ascending track 142, from 2024-11-19 to 2026-11-06. Each track uses a dry-season reference within the requested 2024-11-15 to 2026-11-15 window; four-year seasonal normalization and secondary opinions are not included.
- Flag rule: a barangay is flagged when its high-confidence flood area is at least 2 ha and 3x its same-track dry-season noise, with at least 25% of it visible. A more lenient trial (1 ha, 2x, 15%) raised flagged barangay-passes from 294 to 431 and was reverted as too permissive; it is not published.
- The selection is a focused set with documented flood exposure, not a verified ranking of every Laguna municipality: [IGES studied the Pila–Victoria subwatershed](https://www.iges.or.jp/en/pub/land-cover-change-and-flood-extent-pila/en), and a [UP Los Baños student assessment identified flood-prone Santa Cruz barangays](https://ersg.uplb.edu.ph/student-projects/areas-that-are-high-risk-of-flooding-in-santa-cruz-laguna/). Bay was the existing study area.
- National and other province boundary extracts prepared during an earlier scope are retained as data, but the viewer and current processing cover Laguna only.
- The earlier Bay-only preview remains in `floods/0403402000/` but is no longer the active index. It was generated by `scripts/publish_bay_primary.py` from `/tmp/mata-floods/classes_old.npz` and `manifest_old.json`, with 84 track-32 passes and no second opinions or inferred durations.
- The Copernicus Data Space inventory identified nine acquisitions at the catalogue check point that were not processed into replay frames. It is a point-based check, not proof that all acquisitions covering every municipality were found; those missing observations are not dry frames.
- One `NotGeoreferencedWarning` was emitted during parallel track-142 dry-reference processing. Seven ascending dry-season source slices spot-checked afterward had a CRS and non-identity geotransform; the warning's exact source was not isolated, so it remains a data-quality limitation.
- The 2025-04-02 ascending pass produced approximately 832 ha of high-confidence candidate pixels across the four municipalities, unusually high compared with nearby ascending dry-season passes. None of the four municipalities' barangays cleared the three-times-noise flag on that pass. DAHITI recorded a falling lake level around that date; these comparisons do not establish whether the candidate pixels were floodwater. Ground validation and the deferred seasonal baseline are needed.

## Known limits

- Terrain AOI is Laguna barangay bounds plus 0.08 degrees, mosaicked from four GLO-30 tiles. Smoothing reduces summit heights; the terrain is not survey-grade.
- Of 1,570 building features sampled at z15 inside Bay, 243 (about 15 %) carry a `height`. The rest get a fixed schematic height in the app. OSM building coverage in Bay is sparse; missing footprints are unknown, not empty land.
- Barangays 0403402007 and 0403402008 do not exist in the source; 15 barangays are listed. Not yet checked against the PSA list.
- Flood frames show open water only. WorldCover built-up and tree cover is radar-blind (C-band returns walls and canopy, not the water around them) and is unknown, not dry: Bitin, Santa Cruz, Tranca and both poblacions are under 25 % visible and stay unknown on every pass. Bay lakeshore homes reported waist-deep from July to December 2025 (Rappler, 30 Sep 2025) do not appear as flood for this reason.
- Water on WorldCover cropland or wetland that is not connected to the lake is low confidence and excluded from durations: in Bay this is most of the signal (inland paddies 1 to 5 km from the shore), so paddy cycles and rain-flooded paddies cannot be told apart.
- Boundaries are reference geometry, not a legal determination.
